About Kaayko

Paddling Out

Know before you go

Live paddle-condition scores for real lakes. Wind, water temperature, UV and rain fold into one number from 1 to 5 — check it before you drive out.

Guidance, not clearance. The model is trained on real paddler ratings, but it cannot see your skill, your equipment, or the launch site. Wear a life jacket every time, whatever the number says.

An open-source AI workstation that runs entirely on a Mac. Every image in this chapter was rendered on-device — no cloud, no subscription, no upload.

Store

Invite-only goods

A small run of printed shirts and totes. The store opens with an invite code, and vote counts decide which designs get printed next.

How to get in. Ask for a code — it is not exclusivity, it is just how small this is. A human sends every invite.

kaay.link

QR codes you can change after they are printed

A code made in seconds and pointed anywhere, with every scan visible as it happens. No account, and the code can be re-pointed long after the poster is on the wall.

The sticker outlives the campaign. A printed code cannot be corrected, so the one thing it must carry is an address you will still own in three years. Kaayko keeps the code working; where it goes stays your decision to keep making.

Alumni

A school's own alumni portal

A directory that stays current because the alumni keep it current — searchable by batch, employer and city — with mentorship proposed by a teacher who knows both sides, and reunions people actually turn up to.

Giving is built and not switched on. Card and UPI payment is written and in testing, and no school gets it until it has been proven end to end. Data stays in India, on Indian infrastructure.

Made with care

Why are you not using local AI to create art? Why are you not buying this overpriced t-shirt? Why does the QR code on a thousand posters go to a dead page? Why is there no place for alumni to give back? Kaayko has answers to problems people are too lazy to ask about.
