      Kaayko Store

      Two kinds of thing,
      printed on purpose.

      Kaayko began with one question — is the water worth it today. Everything since has been an answer to a second one: what do you wear while you find out.

      The animals

      Drawn by hand, one at a time, mostly Indian and mostly in trouble. There are a few hundred Bengal floricans left in the world. You will almost certainly never see a snow leopard. We put them on the things you carry every day, because a bag you use is harder to forget than a photograph you scrolled past.

      Totes and magnets. No stock art, no photographs.

      The shirts

      These are not that. These are jokes, told straight and printed large: one word that promises transcendence and delivers cotton, a philosopher who ran an empire, a man who could not hear his own applause. If a shirt has to explain itself it has already failed.

      Every tagline in the shop is on the shirt. Try the game below.

      How this works

      • Printed to order. Nothing is made until you buy it, so nothing is made to be thrown away.
      • Two things to a bag. The basket stops at two items. That is not a bug, and we are not going to fix it.
      • Thirty days to change your mind. Unworn, unwashed, refunded in full. The details.
      • Small runs. A design that stops selling stops being printed, and does not come back as a sale.
