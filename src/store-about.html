      Small cart, big impact

      For too long humans have been adding item after items to their cart. Not at Kaayko. We limit your purchases to two items per bag. No sign ups, no accounts, no passwords. You are a guest, nothing more.

      No account, no relationship

      We will not remember your birthday. We will not email you on it. You buy the thing, the thing arrives, and we go back to being strangers. Other shops call this a churn problem. We call it Tuesday.

      You are not the product. Neither is your inbox.
