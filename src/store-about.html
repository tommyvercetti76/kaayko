      Style Your Soul.
      Soul sold separately.

      Welcome. You have found the only shop on the internet actively trying to sell you less. We could move eleven of these to you before lunch. We have decided on two. Somewhere a growth consultant just woke up cold and does not know why.

      Two. That is the entire basket.

      Not two of each. Two. The basket stops, and it will keep stopping no matter how badly you want to hand us money. Everywhere else is engineered so you forget where your wallet ends and your personality begins. Buy three, get the fourth free. Spend twelve more to unlock shipping you did not want on a thing you did not need, to reach a tier that does not exist, for a badge nobody will ever see.

      This is not scarcity marketing. Scarcity marketing is when we pretend to run out. We are not pretending. We have simply been to your house.

      The cap is not a bug. We will not be fixing it.

      No account. No password. No relationship.

      Guest checkout, and only guest checkout. No profile. No loyalty tier. No welcome back in a typeface we licensed specifically to sound like it cares. We are not building a version of you to sell to somebody else.

      We do not want to know what you nearly bought at two in the morning. We will not email you in six weeks pretending we noticed you were gone, because we will not have noticed, because we were outside. You buy the thing, the thing arrives, we go back to being strangers. This used to be called shopping.

      You are not the product. Neither is your inbox.

      Representation, for things that cannot advertise

      Everything printed here stands in for something with no marketing budget and no say in the matter. Living, non-living, or somewhere between. Thought counts. Objects count. Animals count, and they have been counting for some time now, downward. If it deserved a monument and got a landfill, it is a candidate.

      Fetching the evidence…

      The past had standards. We checked. Twice.

      The person who makes these believes the world used to be better made. Not kinder. Better made. A stamp hand-engraved at the India Security Press in 1953 still holds up enlarged four times at 3600 pixels, seventy years and three currencies later. Try that with a push notification. Try that with anything that shipped this quarter.

      So the shop looks backwards deliberately. Not because the past was innocent, but because it was willing to take its time, sign its work, and be looked at closely by strangers who were not born yet.

      As for the future: shut up, sit down and relax. Let the instincts take over. Nature has run this operation for four billion years with no quarterly review, no growth target and no engagement data, and it has never once needed to A/B test a sunset.

      A cat toy for your entertainment

      Two machines that sell you nothing. No reading, no questions, no clever answer to look up — just the old kind of game, where your hands either do it or they do not. One tap, one hold. Built to be lost quickly and played again anyway.

      The same two sit on every magnet and every bottle in the shop, and clearing one there is worth 2% off. Codes last an hour from the moment you win them, used or not. There is a third game at the bag, but that one you have to talk your way into.
