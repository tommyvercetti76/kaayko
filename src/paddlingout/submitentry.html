What gets an entry declined
  • No public access — private docks, fenced ramps, permit-only shorelines.
  • Photos that are not yours or do not show the water and launch.
  • A launch that is already on the map, or a pin far from the real put-in.
  • Conditions we cannot forecast safely (dams, spillways, active rapids).
