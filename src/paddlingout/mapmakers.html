    The rules

    Effective 28 October 2026. Tap one to open it.

    Who can take part

    Anyone may add a lake. To be picked you must be 18 or older and have given an email. Pieces ship to US addresses only for now.

    How picks are made

    Kaayko picks up to ten new lakes each calendar month, judged on the photos, the accuracy of the pin and how useful the note is. Kaayko’s choice is final. A month may have fewer than ten picks, or none. Only new lakes that Kaayko approves count; suggested edits do not.

    How often

    One pick per person per month, however many lakes you add, and one piece per shipping address per month. Entries that look like one person under several emails are checked before a pick is made.

    Hearing from us

    We email the people picked within seven days of the month’s end. The email carries a personal link that works once. Claim your piece with it within 30 days, or it lapses.

    The piece

    A Kaayko item made for Mapmakers, worth under $25. It cannot be exchanged for money or for another item.

    Nothing to buy

    No purchase or payment is needed to take part or to receive a piece. Shipping is included.

    Your photos and your lake

    You confirm the photos are yours and the launch is open to the public. You allow Kaayko to show your photos, pin, note and chosen name on Paddling Out. You can ask for your name to come off at any time: help@kaayko.com.

    Changes

    Kaayko may change or end the monthly picks at any time. Kaayko may refuse or withdraw a pick that breaks these rules, for example photos that are not yours or a launch that is not public.

    Not a safety endorsement

    A lake on Paddling Out is not a statement that it is safe to paddle. Check the conditions and the local rules before you go.
