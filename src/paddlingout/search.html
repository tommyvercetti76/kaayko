Search resolves any named lake, river or reservoir by city, state or postcode and returns a live Paddle Score for it — wind speed and gusts, air temperature, UV index, cloud cover and precipitation folded into one number from 1 (Danger) to 5 (Excellent), with fixed safety rules applied on top.

Coverage is not the same as curation. Twenty-one spots are hand-picked, and seventeen of them are written up in detail, with notes on what drives the score at each one and what the model cannot see there. Everywhere else returns a score from weather data alone. Browse the 22 spots, or read how the Paddle Score is built.

Find water near you

1Type any lake, river or city — you get a live Paddle Score for it.
2Tap anywhere on the map to search there, or drag it and tap “Search this area”.
3Tap any pin to open that lake’s forecast.

5Not on the map yet? Tap Add this lake on a result, or the Add a lake tab — the name and pin come with you.
