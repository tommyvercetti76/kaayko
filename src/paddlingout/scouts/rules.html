Kaayko Scouts

The rules

Effective 6 November 2026. Ten rules. The button says each one in a child’s words.

Who can take part

Anyone, anywhere. No sign-up, no age limit. Want the code or a Scout page? Give an email with the spot.

What earns a code

Every new spot Kaayko approves that had an email on it. One code a spot. Suggested edits and reports earn none.

The code

20% off one order of Kaayko’s own pieces at kaay.store. One use, within 180 days of sending, typed at the bag. Not valid on Kreators’ pieces, one code an order, no cash value.

How it reaches you

In the email that says your spot is live, and under that spot on your Scout page. Lost both? Write to help@kaayko.com with the spot’s name.

What we check

A person looks at every entry: a public launch, the pin on the launch, your own photos of the water, a name that reads like a place, and not already on the map. Kaayko’s decision is final. A declined entry gets an email, with the reason when there is one. Review has no fixed time.

Your name on the spot

Optional. Shown as “Added by” on the spot’s page. Take it off any time on your Scout page, or write to help@kaayko.com. We may shorten or correct it.

Your photos and your spot

You confirm the photos are yours and the launch is public, and you let Kaayko show your photos, pin, note and name on Paddling Out. To take a live spot down, ask on your Scout page: others may rely on it, so a person decides and writes to you either way.

Your Scout page

Your first entry with an email gets you a Scout code. With your email and that code, kaay.link/m lets you see everything you sent, take your name off, withdraw a spot still in review (never published; its photos and your details deleted, see Privacy), ask for a live spot to come down, or get a new code, which ends the old one. Keep the code private. At most six code emails a day. Codes starting MK still work.

Changes

Kaayko may change or end the thank-you. A code already sent works for its 180 days, and a monthly pick made before 6 November 2026 is honoured. Kaayko may refuse or take down a spot that breaks these rules.

Not a safety endorsement

A spot on Paddling Out is not a statement that it is safe to paddle. A paddler’s report is what they say they saw: checked before it shows, never confirmed, and not permission to paddle. No report does not mean nothing is there. Check conditions and local rules first.
